# Kindergarten Curriculum (2026)

**Kindergarten Curriculum (2026)** is the Kindergarten Kindergarten program in [Kindergarten](../subjects/kindergarten.md).

## Before you take this course

This course has no formal prerequisites.

## What students learn

The learning is organized into 4 strands: Foundations of Language and Mathematics, Problem Solving and Innovating, Self-Regulation and Well-Being, and Belonging and Contributing.

### A. Foundations of Language and Mathematics

*As students progress through Kindergarten, they:*

**A1 Oral and Non-Verbal Communication.** communicate [orally](../glossary/oral-communication.md) and [non-verbally](../glossary/non-verbal-communication.md)

- listen and respond
- use [language conventions](../glossary/language-conventions-db132687.md) and non-verbal communication
- understand and use new words and develop [morphological awareness](../glossary/morphological-awareness.md)

<sub>The source curriculum adds 2 teacher prompts, examples, and instructional notes for these expectations.</sub>

**A2 Foundations for Reading and Writing.** apply early reading and writing skills

- name upper- and lowercase letters (both in and out of order)
- use proper formation patterns to write letters
- isolate and blend [phonemes](../glossary/phoneme-96cbc559.md), and segment simple words into phonemes
- identify simple [grapheme-phoneme correspondences](../glossary/grapheme-phoneme-correspondence-2372bfae.md)
- read and spell simple words using [phonemic awareness](../glossary/phonemic-awareness.md) and [phonics](../glossary/phonics-580d6709.md) knowledge
- read short sentences [fluently](../glossary/fluency-dabd509d.md), including those with explicitly taught words, with increasing accuracy, to support [comprehension](../glossary/comprehension-a2b97c58.md)

**A3 Understanding and Responding to Texts.** understand and respond to a variety of [texts](../glossary/text-d9f68102.md) by diverse creators from Canada and by diverse First Nations, Métis, and Inuit creators

- identify purposes for choosing different texts
- apply knowledge of words, [sentence structure](../glossary/sentence-structure.md), and [punctuation](../glossary/punctuation.md)
- read and understand simple texts, using background knowledge and beginning knowledge of words, grammar, and sentence structure
- express personal thoughts and feelings about texts
- make predictions about unfamiliar texts, using background knowledge
- make connections to a variety of texts

<sub>The source curriculum adds 6 teacher prompts, examples, and instructional notes for these expectations.</sub>

**A4 Expressing Ideas and Creating Texts.** create [texts](../glossary/text-d9f68102.md) using foundational language knowledge and skills

- use knowledge of letters, words, and sentences, with pictures and symbols, to create simple texts
- use classroom writing resources to support creating simple texts

<sub>The source curriculum adds 2 teacher prompts, examples, and instructional notes for these expectations.</sub>

**A5 The Mathematical Processes.** apply the mathematical processes (i.e., problem solving; reasoning and proving; reflecting; connecting; communicating; representing; and selecting [tools](../glossary/tools-math.md) and strategies)

**A6 Number Sense and Operations.** use numbers and [operations](../glossary/operation.md)

- explore what numbers are used for
- understand when [counting](../glossary/count.md) forward, the quantity gets bigger and when counting backward, the quantity gets smaller
- identify and write numbers to 20
- [subitize](../glossary/subitizing-73c9199c.md) quantities to 5
- [compose](../glossary/compose-math.md) and [decompose](../glossary/decompose-math.md) quantities to 10
- understand the concept of more than, the same (equal), or less than
- [estimate](../glossary/estimate.md) the number of objects in small collections and check by counting
- count to 20 by 1s, starting from any number
- solve [equal-share problems](../glossary/equal-share-problem.md) using concrete materials
- describe relationships between any two numbers from 0 to 20
- recall [addition](../glossary/addition.md) [facts](../glossary/math-facts.md) to 5 and related [subtraction](../glossary/subtraction.md) facts
- solve situations involving addition and subtraction to 10 using concrete materials
- identify and explore the value of Canadian coins to 25¢ and coins and bills to $10

<sub>The source curriculum adds 13 teacher prompts, examples, and instructional notes for these expectations.</sub>

**A7 Patterns and Relationships.** investigate [repeating patterns](../glossary/repeating-pattern.md)

- identify, describe, and create [patterns](../glossary/pattern-e98d801e.md)
- identify the [core](../glossary/pattern-core.md) of a repeating pattern and describe why it is important
- show that patterns can be [represented](../glossary/representation-math.md) in different ways because the core remains the same
- extend patterns in both directions and identify any missing [elements](../glossary/element-641d02bc.md)

<sub>The source curriculum adds 4 teacher prompts, examples, and instructional notes for these expectations.</sub>

**A8 Data Literacy and Probability.** collect and display [data](../glossary/data-43a42914.md) to answer questions and communicate information, and explore [probability](../glossary/probability.md) in everyday contexts

- sort objects or data using one [attribute](../glossary/attribute.md)
- collect and record data to answer questions of interest, and display it in [concrete graphs](../glossary/concrete-graph.md) or picture graphs
- respond to data in concrete graphs and picture graphs by interpreting it and drawing conclusions
- describe how likely familiar [events](../glossary/event-9049bcdf.md) are to happen

<sub>The source curriculum adds 4 teacher prompts, examples, and instructional notes for these expectations.</sub>

**A9 Geometric and Spatial Reasoning.** describe [two-dimensional shapes](../glossary/two-dimensional-shape.md) and [three-dimensional objects](../glossary/three-dimensional-object-e7630a4b.md), and the location and movement of people and objects

- identify, [sort](../glossary/sort.md), and [compare](../glossary/compare.md) the most common two-dimensional shapes, including [circle](../glossary/circle-cc2ff9f4.md), [hexagon](../glossary/hexagon.md), [rhombus](../glossary/rhombus.md), and [trapezoid](../glossary/trapezoid.md)
- identify, sort, and compare the most common three-dimensional objects, including  [cube](../glossary/cube.md), [sphere](../glossary/sphere.md), [prisms](../glossary/prism.md), [cone](../glossary/cone.md), and [pyramid](../glossary/pyramid.md)
- in structures they have made, identify three-dimensional objects and two-dimensional shapes
- construct two-dimensional pictures and three-dimensional structures with matching halves
- put two-dimensional shapes together to make other shapes, break them into smaller shapes, and put them together in different ways
- describe basic spatial [relationships](../glossary/relationship-f5b5e0bf.md) and movements, and visualize objects from different [perspectives](../glossary/perspective-da248d6f.md)
- give and follow directions for moving from one location to another

<sub>The source curriculum adds 7 teacher prompts, examples, and instructional notes for these expectations.</sub>

**A10 Measurement.** describe and [compare](../glossary/compare.md) the length, area, [mass](../glossary/mass.md), or [capacity](../glossary/capacity-ed6fdb5b.md) of a variety of objects

- describe different ways that objects can be measured
- compare the length, area, mass, and capacity of objects using [concrete materials](../glossary/concrete-materials.md)

<sub>The source curriculum adds 2 teacher prompts, examples, and instructional notes for these expectations.</sub>


*Transferable skills developed in this strand: Communication, Collaboration, Critical Thinking & Problem Solving, Digital Literacy, Global Citizenship and Sustainability, Innovation, Creativity & Entrepreneurship.*

### B. Problem Solving and Innovating

*As students progress through Kindergarten, they:*

**B11 Coding Skills.** use foundational coding concepts and skills

- use directional and positional language to create and follow instructions involving movement
- test and refine instructions
- communicate and record step-by-step instructions using symbols, words, or pictures

<sub>The source curriculum adds 3 teacher prompts, examples, and instructional notes for these expectations.</sub>

**B12 Scientific Investigation and Engineering Design.** use an inquiry process to engage in scientific investigations and to design, build, and test models and structures

- state problems and pose questions
- make predictions and observations while exploring, investigating, and designing
- select materials and tools to carry out investigations and to construct models and structures
- draw conclusions and communicate results
- design, build, and test models and structures
- follow safety rules to help prevent injuries, including concussion, and demonstrate an understanding of the importance of these practices

<sub>The source curriculum adds 6 teacher prompts, examples, and instructional notes for these expectations.</sub>

**B13 Exploring Environments.** investigate natural and built environments

- ask questions about and describe some natural occurrences
- sort and classify groups of living and non-living things
- compare patterns in natural and built environments

<sub>The source curriculum adds 3 teacher prompts, examples, and instructional notes for these expectations.</sub>


*Transferable skills developed in this strand: Collaboration, Critical Thinking & Problem Solving, Innovation, Creativity & Entrepreneurship, Communication, Global Citizenship and Sustainability.*

### C. Self-Regulation and Well-Being

*As students progress through Kindergarten, they:*

**C14 Social Skills.** identify and use social skills in play and other contexts

- act and talk with peers and adults by expressing and accepting positive messages
- show the ability to take turns
- demonstrate an awareness of ways of making and keeping friends
- use problem-solving skills in social situations

<sub>The source curriculum adds 4 teacher prompts, examples, and instructional notes for these expectations.</sub>

**C15 Self-Regulation.** demonstrate independence, self-regulation, and a willingness to take on responsibility

- demonstrate self-reliance and a sense of responsibility
- demonstrate a willingness to try new experiences and to adapt to new situations
- demonstrate self-motivation, initiative, and confidence in their approach to learning
- demonstrate self-control and adapt behaviour to different contexts
- develop empathy for others, and acknowledge and respond to each other’s feelings

<sub>The source curriculum adds 5 teacher prompts, examples, and instructional notes for these expectations.</sub>

**C16 Understanding Health Concepts.** demonstrate an awareness of their own health and well-being

- demonstrate an understanding of the effects of healthy, active living on the mind and body
- investigate the benefits of nutritious foods and explore ways of ensuring healthy eating
- practise and discuss appropriate personal hygiene that promotes personal, family, and community health
- discuss what action to take when they feel unsafe or uncomfortable, in person or online, and when and how to seek assistance
- discuss and demonstrate in play what makes them happy and unhappy, and why

<sub>The source curriculum adds 3 teacher prompts, examples, and instructional notes for these expectations.</sub>

**C17 Active Participation.** participate actively and regularly in a variety of activities that require the application of [movement concepts](../glossary/movement-concepts.md)

- participate actively in creative movement and other daily physical activities
- demonstrate persistence while engaged in activities that require the use of both large and small muscles
- demonstrate strategies for engaging in cooperative play in a variety of games and activities

<sub>The source curriculum adds 2 teacher prompts, examples, and instructional notes for these expectations.</sub>

**C18 Movement Skills and Concepts.** develop [movement skills](../glossary/movement-skills.md) and [concepts](../glossary/movement-concepts.md) as they use their growing bodies to move in a variety of ways and in a variety of contexts

- demonstrate [spatial awareness](../glossary/movement-concepts.md) in activities that require the use of large muscles
- demonstrate control of large muscles with and without equipment
- demonstrate balance, whole-body and hand-eye coordination, and flexibility in movement
- demonstrate control of small muscles while working in a variety of learning areas and when using a variety of materials or equipment
- demonstrate spatial awareness by doing activities that require the use of small muscles

<sub>The source curriculum adds 3 teacher prompts, examples, and instructional notes for these expectations.</sub>


*Transferable skills developed in this strand: Communication, Collaboration, Critical Thinking & Problem Solving, Self-Directed Learning, Digital Literacy.*

### D. Belonging and Contributing

*As students progress through Kindergarten, they:*

**D19 Identity and Self-Image.** demonstrate a sense of identity and a positive self-image

- recognize personal interests, strengths, and accomplishments
- identify and talk about their own interests and preferences
- express their thoughts and share experiences

<sub>The source curriculum adds 1 teacher prompt, example, and instructional note for these expectations.</sub>

**D20 Understanding Perspectives and Canadian Identity.** show understanding of the multiple [perspectives](../glossary/perspective-da248d6f.md) and diversity among individuals, groups, and communities in Canada, and identify areas of connection

- understand that everyone belongs to multiple groups and communities, and that these groups and communities together shape Canadian identity
- describe, both verbally and non-verbally, ways in which they contribute to the various groups to which they belong
- demonstrate respect for different perspectives and identify areas of connection

<sub>The source curriculum adds 2 teacher prompts, examples, and instructional notes for these expectations.</sub>

**D21 Self-Confidence.** recognize [bias](../glossary/bias-420f50ab.md) in ideas and develop the self-confidence to stand up for themselves and others against prejudice and discrimination

- stand up for themselves, and apply behaviours that enhance their own and others’ well-being
- [think critically](../glossary/critical-thinking.md) about fair/unfair and biased behaviour towards both themselves and others, and act with compassion and kindness
- recognize discriminatory and inequitable practices and behaviours and respond appropriately

<sub>The source curriculum adds 1 teacher prompt, example, and instructional note for these expectations.</sub>

**D22 People and Communities.** demonstrate an awareness of their surroundings

- recognize people in their community and talk about what they do
- recognize places and buildings within their community, both natural and human-made, and talk about their functions

<sub>The source curriculum adds 2 teacher prompts, examples, and instructional notes for these expectations.</sub>

**D23 Respect and Care for Nature.** demonstrate an understanding of the natural world and the need to care for and respect the environment

- identify similarities and differences between local environments
- describe what would happen if something in the local environment changed
- describe ways in which they care for and respect the environment

<sub>The source curriculum adds 3 teacher prompts, examples, and instructional notes for these expectations.</sub>

**D24 Responding to Art.** respond to a variety of forms of dance, drama, music, and visual arts from various cultures and communities, including using their language and mathematics knowledge and skills

- express their responses to dance and drama
- respond to [texts](../glossary/text-d9f68102.md) and art forms from various cultures and communities through classroom drama activities and make connections to their own experiences
- express their responses to music by moving, by making connections to their own experiences, or by talking about the musical form

<sub>The source curriculum adds 3 teacher prompts, examples, and instructional notes for these expectations.</sub>

**D25 Exploring and Creating Art.** explore and create a variety of art forms, including dance, drama, music, and visual arts

- explore the elements of dance, drama, music, and [design](../glossary/design-visual-arts.md)
- experiment with different materials, techniques, and processes used in a variety of art forms
- use problem-solving skills and imagination to create forms of art
- communicate ideas through dance, drama, music, and visual arts

<sub>The source curriculum adds 4 teacher prompts, examples, and instructional notes for these expectations.</sub>


*Transferable skills developed in this strand: Self-Directed Learning, Communication, Global Citizenship and Sustainability, Collaboration, Critical Thinking & Problem Solving, Innovation, Creativity & Entrepreneurship.*
