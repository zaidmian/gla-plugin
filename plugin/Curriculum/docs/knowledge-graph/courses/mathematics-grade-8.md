# Mathematics (Grade 8)

**Mathematics** is the Grade 8 Mathematics program in [Mathematics](../subjects/mathematics.md).

Here you will find the expectations and learning for the six areas of study for Grade 8 Mathematics.

## Before you take this course

This course has no formal prerequisites.

## What students learn

The learning is organized into 6 strands: Social-Emotional Learning (SEL) Skills in Mathematics and the Mathematical Processes, Number, Algebra, Data, Spatial Sense, and Financial Literacy.

### A. Social-Emotional Learning (SEL) Skills in Mathematics and the Mathematical Processes

*Throughout this grade, in order to promote a positive identity as a math learner, to foster well-being and the ability to learn, build resilience, and thrive, students will:*

**A1 Social-Emotional Learning (SEL) Skills and the Mathematical Processes.** apply, to the best of their ability, a variety of social-emotional learning skills to support their use of the mathematical processes and their learning in connection with the expectations in the other five strands of the mathematics curriculum


### B. Number

*By the end of Grade 8, students will:*

**B1 Number Sense.** demonstrate an understanding of numbers and make connections to the way numbers are used in everyday life

- [represent](../glossary/represent.md) and compare very large and very small numbers, including through the use of [scientific notation](../glossary/scientific-notation.md), and describe various ways they are used in everyday life
- describe, compare, and order numbers in the [real number](../glossary/real-numbers.md) system (rational and irrational numbers), separately and in combination, in various contexts
- estimate and calculate [square roots](../glossary/square-root-of-a-number.md), in various contexts
- use [fractions](../glossary/fraction.md), [decimal numbers](../glossary/decimal-number.md), and [percents](../glossary/percent.md), including percents of more than 100% or less than 1%, interchangeably and flexibly to solve a variety of problems

<sub>The source curriculum adds 15 teacher prompts, examples, and instructional notes for these expectations.</sub>

**B2 Operations.** use knowledge of numbers and operations to solve mathematical problems encountered in everyday life

- use the properties and [order of operations](../glossary/order-of-operations.md), and the [relationships](../glossary/relationship-f5b5e0bf.md) between operations, to solve problems involving [rational numbers](../glossary/rational-number.md), [ratios](../glossary/ratio.md), [rates](../glossary/rate.md), and percents, including those requiring multiple steps or multiple operations
- understand and recall commonly used square numbers and their square roots
- use [mental math strategies](../glossary/mental-math-strategies.md) to multiply and divide [whole numbers](../glossary/whole-number.md) and decimal numbers up to thousandths by [powers of ten](../glossary/power-of-ten.md), and explain the strategies used
- add and subtract [integers](../glossary/integer.md), using appropriate strategies, in various contexts
- add and subtract fractions, using appropriate strategies, in various contexts
- multiply and divide fractions by fractions, as well as by whole numbers and mixed numbers, in various contexts
- multiply and divide integers, using appropriate strategies, in various contexts
- compare [proportional](../glossary/proportional.md) situations and determine unknown values in proportional situations, and apply [proportional reasoning](../glossary/proportional-reasoning.md) to solve problems in various contexts

<sub>The source curriculum adds 36 teacher prompts, examples, and instructional notes for these expectations.</sub>


*Transferable skills developed in this strand: Critical Thinking & Problem Solving, Communication.*

### C. Algebra

*By the end of Grade 8, students will:*

**C1 Patterns and Relationships.** identify, describe, extend, create, and make predictions about a variety of patterns, including those found in real-life contexts

- identify and compare a variety of [repeating](../glossary/repeating-pattern-5a556eeb.md), [growing](../glossary/growing-pattern.md), and [shrinking patterns](../glossary/shrinking-pattern-1b765839.md), including patterns found in real-life contexts, and compare [linear](../glossary/linear-pattern.md) growing and shrinking patterns on the basis of their [constant rates](../glossary/constant-rate.md) and initial values
- create and [translate](../glossary/translate-6bbeec99.md) repeating, growing, and shrinking patterns involving rational numbers using various [representations](../glossary/representation.md), including [algebraic expressions](../glossary/algebraic-expression.md) and [equations](../glossary/equation.md) for linear growing and shrinking patterns
- determine [pattern rules](../glossary/pattern-rule.md) and use them to extend patterns, make and justify predictions, and identify missing [elements](../glossary/element.md) in growing and shrinking patterns involving rational numbers, and use algebraic representations of the pattern rules to solve for unknown values in linear growing and shrinking patterns
- create and describe patterns to illustrate [relationships](../glossary/relationship-f5b5e0bf.md) among rational numbers

<sub>The source curriculum adds 18 teacher prompts, examples, and instructional notes for these expectations.</sub>

**C2 Equations and Inequalities.** demonstrate an understanding of variables, expressions, equations, and inequalities, and apply this understanding in various contexts

- add and subtract [monomials](../glossary/monomial.md) with a degree of 1, and add [binomials](../glossary/binomial.md) with a degree of 1 that involve integers, using tools
- [evaluate](../glossary/evaluate.md) algebraic expressions that involve rational numbers
- solve equations that involve multiple [terms](../glossary/term-b0dc5ad8.md), integers, and decimal numbers in various contexts, and verify solutions
- solve [inequalities](../glossary/inequality.md) that involve integers, and verify and graph the solutions

<sub>The source curriculum adds 15 teacher prompts, examples, and instructional notes for these expectations.</sub>

**C3 Coding.** solve problems and create computational representations of mathematical situations using coding concepts and skills

- solve problems and create [computational representations](../glossary/computational-representations.md) of mathematical situations by writing and [executing code](../glossary/execute-coding.md), including [code](../glossary/code.md) that involves the analysis of data in order to inform and communicate decisions
- read and alter existing code involving the analysis of data in order to inform and communicate decisions, and describe how changes to the code affect the [outcomes](../glossary/outcome-coding.md) and the efficiency of the code

<sub>The source curriculum adds 8 teacher prompts, examples, and instructional notes for these expectations.</sub>

**C4 Mathematical Modelling.** apply the process of mathematical modelling to represent, analyse, make predictions, and provide insight into real-life situations 
 
 This overall expectation has no specific expectations. [Mathematical modelling](../glossary/mathematical-modelling.md) is an iterative and interconnected process that is applied to various contexts, allowing students to bring in learning from other strands. Students’ demonstration of the process of mathematical modelling, as they apply concepts and skills learned in other strands, is assessed and evaluated. 
 Read more about the mathematical modelling process.


*Transferable skills developed in this strand: Critical Thinking & Problem Solving, Communication, Digital Literacy.*

### D. Data

*By the end of Grade 8, students will:*

**D1 Data Literacy.** manage, analyse, and use data to make convincing arguments and informed decisions, in various contexts drawn from real life

- identify situations involving one-variable data and situations involving [two-variable data](../glossary/two-variable-data.md), and explain when each type of data is needed
- collect [continuous data](../glossary/continuous-data.md) to answer [questions of interest](../glossary/question-of-interest.md) involving two variables, and organize the data sets as appropriate in a [table of values](../glossary/table-of-values.md)
- select from among a variety of graphs, including [scatter plots](../glossary/scatter-plot.md), the type of graph best suited to represent various sets of data; display the data in the graphs with proper sources, titles, and labels, and appropriate scales; and justify their choice of graphs
- create an [infographic](../glossary/infographic.md) about a data set, representing the data in appropriate ways, including in tables and scatter plots, and incorporating any other relevant information that helps to tell a story about the data
- use mathematical language, including the terms “strong”, “weak”, “none”, “positive”, and “negative”, to describe the relationship between two variables for various data sets with and without [outliers](../glossary/outlier.md)
- analyse different sets of data presented in various ways, including in scatter plots and in [misleading graphs](../glossary/misleading-graph.md), by asking and answering questions about the data, challenging preconceived notions, and drawing conclusions, then make convincing arguments and informed decisions

<sub>The source curriculum adds 20 teacher prompts, examples, and instructional notes for these expectations.</sub>

**D2 Probability.** describe the likelihood that events will happen, and use that information to make predictions

- solve various problems that involve [probability](../glossary/probability.md), using appropriate tools and strategies, including [Venn](../glossary/venn-diagram-ca7ba686.md) and [tree diagrams](../glossary/tree-diagram.md)
- determine and compare the [theoretical](../glossary/theoretical-probability.md) and [experimental probabilities](../glossary/experimental-probability.md) of multiple [independent events](../glossary/independent-events.md) happening and of multiple [dependent events](../glossary/dependent-events.md) happening

<sub>The source curriculum adds 7 teacher prompts, examples, and instructional notes for these expectations.</sub>


*Transferable skills developed in this strand: Critical Thinking & Problem Solving, Digital Literacy, Innovation, Creativity & Entrepreneurship, Communication.*

### E. Spatial Sense

*By the end of Grade 8, students will:*

**E1 Geometric and Spatial Reasoning.** describe and represent shape, location, and movement by applying [geometric properties](../glossary/geometric-property.md) and spatial relationships in order to navigate the world around them

- identify geometric properties of tessellating shapes and identify the [transformations](../glossary/transformation-c2e94529.md) that occur in the [tessellations](../glossary/tessellation.md)
- make objects and models using appropriate scales, given their top, front, and side views or their [perspective views](../glossary/perspective-view.md)
- use [scale drawings](../glossary/scale-drawing.md) to calculate actual lengths and areas, and reproduce scale drawings at different [ratios](../glossary/ratio.md)
- describe and perform [translations](../glossary/translation-ab11c126.md), [reflections](../glossary/reflection.md), [rotations](../glossary/rotation.md), and [dilations](../glossary/dilation.md) on a [Cartesian plane](../glossary/cartesian-plane.md), and predict the results of these [transformations](../glossary/transformation-c2e94529.md)

<sub>The source curriculum adds 21 teacher prompts, examples, and instructional notes for these expectations.</sub>

**E2 Measurement.** compare, estimate, and determine measurements in various contexts

- represent very large (mega, giga, tera) and very small (micro, nano, pico) metric units using models, base ten relationships, and [exponential notation](../glossary/exponential-notation.md)
- solve problems involving angle properties, including the properties of intersecting and [parallel lines](../glossary/parallel-lines.md) and of polygons
- solve problems involving the [perimeter](../glossary/perimeter.md), [circumference](../glossary/circumference.md), area, [volume](../glossary/volume.md), and [surface area](../glossary/surface-area.md) of composite [two-dimensional shapes](../glossary/two-dimensional-shape.md) and [three-dimensional objects](../glossary/three-dimensional-object-e7630a4b.md), using appropriate formulas
- describe the [Pythagorean relationship](../glossary/pythagorean-relationship.md) using various geometric models, and apply the [theorem](../glossary/pythagorean-theorem.md) to solve problems involving an unknown side length for a given [right triangle](../glossary/right-angle-triangle.md)

<sub>The source curriculum adds 19 teacher prompts, examples, and instructional notes for these expectations.</sub>


*Transferable skills developed in this strand: Critical Thinking & Problem Solving, Communication.*

### F. Financial Literacy

*By the end of Grade 8, students will:*

**F1 Money and Finances.** demonstrate the knowledge and skills needed to make informed financial decisions

- describe some advantages and disadvantages of various [methods of payment](../glossary/method-of-payment.md) that can be used when dealing with multiple [currencies](../glossary/currency.md) and [exchange rates](../glossary/exchange-rate.md)
- create a [financial plan](../glossary/financial-plan.md) to reach a long-term [financial goal](../glossary/financial-goal.md), accounting for [income](../glossary/income.md), [expenses](../glossary/expenses.md), and tax implications
- identify different ways to maintain a [balanced budget](../glossary/balanced-budget.md), and use appropriate tools to track all income and [spending](../glossary/spending.md), for several different scenarios
- determine the growth of [simple](../glossary/simple-interest.md) and [compound interest](../glossary/compound-interest.md) at various rates using digital tools, and explain the impact interest has on long-term financial planning
- compare various ways for consumers to get more value for their money when spending, including taking advantage of sales and customer [loyalty](../glossary/loyalty-program.md) and [incentive programs](../glossary/incentive.md), and determine the best choice for different scenarios
- compare interest rates, annual [fees](../glossary/fees.md), and rewards and other incentives offered by various [credit card](../glossary/credit-card.md) companies and [consumer contracts](../glossary/consumer-contract.md) to determine the best value and the best choice for different scenarios

<sub>The source curriculum adds 24 teacher prompts, examples, and instructional notes for these expectations.</sub>


*Transferable skills developed in this strand: Critical Thinking & Problem Solving, Global Citizenship and Sustainability, Innovation, Creativity & Entrepreneurship.*
