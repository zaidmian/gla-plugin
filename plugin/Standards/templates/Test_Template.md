## Structure (the parts in course.yaml `test_parts`, A and B by default)

<Teacher-only. The generated student handout drops this section and the companion list below.>

- **Part A, Multiple choice** (~40–50% of marks): one mark each, 4 options, exactly one correct.
- **Part B, Short answer** (~50–60% of marks in a two-part test): written items, `**B1. <title> (<marks> marks)**`. The kinds follow the subject: trace-this-code, find-the-error, write-a-fragment and explain (programming); label-the-diagram, a calculation and explain (science); source analysis and short response (history).
- **Later parts** (C, D, only when `test_parts` lists them): written items numbered with their letter (`**C1. ...**`), for example an extended response in history or a lab-data question in science.
